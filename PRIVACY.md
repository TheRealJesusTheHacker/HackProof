## We collect nothing. That's the selling point.

HackProof v1 has:
- **No accounts.** There is nothing to sign up for.
- **No tracking.** No analytics, no cookies for advertising, no fingerprinting.
- **No network calls in the game itself.** The game runs entirely in your browser — scenarios, scoring, and license validation all happen on your device. (The separate buy page fetches a BTC price from CoinGecko so it can show you the current $5 equivalent; that's the only network request in the product, and it sends no personal data.)
- **No data leaves your device.** Your score, your progress, and your license key never get sent to us or anyone else.

## License keys
- Your license key is validated **offline** on your device using cryptography. We never see it.
- The key is kept **only in your browser's memory**, unless you explicitly check "Remember on this device" — then it's stored in that browser's localStorage, still only on your device. You can wipe it anytime with the "Remove license" button.
- License expiry is checked against **your device's clock**. Keep your clock accurate; a wildly wrong clock can make a valid key look expired (or an expired key look valid, until the next check).

## Purchases
If you buy premium, you email us your Bitcoin transaction ID. That email contains whatever you put in it — nothing more. We use it only to verify payment and issue your license key. We don't sell it, share it, or feed it to anything.

## Children
The game is about recognizing scams — useful at any age — but there's no data collection from anyone, child or adult, because there's no data collection at all.

Missouri Guard & PI Licensing Pathways
MODULE OVERVIEW
Level: Beginner | Time: 60 minutes | Prerequisites: None. Orientation only.

LEARNING OBJECTIVES
After this module you can:
- Describe the Missouri/KCMO guard licensing pathway
- Explain the armed endorsement and firearms qualification
- State why de-escalation matters for guards
- Describe the Missouri PI licensing pathway
- Place ASIS APP, CPP, and PCI correctly

LESSON 1 — GUARD PATHWAY: LICENSE, ARMED ENDORSEMENT, TRAINING
In Kansas City, an officer needs a city guard license issued through the Kansas City Police Department's licensing process; requirements elsewhere in Missouri can differ. Pathway: confirm eligibility, apply, complete fingerprinting and a background check, complete any required training steps, and receive the license before working a post. Verify current requirements with the issuing authority.

An armed post adds an armed endorsement on the license, plus firearms qualification through the authority's approved process, kept current. Never carry on duty based on ownership, a carry permit alone, or verbal approval - only the authorization on your license, verified before the shift.

Licensing is the floor. Training also covers de-escalation - voice, posture, distance, and time - and defensive tactics for the role. Sentinel's order: observe, report, create distance, de-escalate.

CASE BREAKDOWN: A venue offers a guard cash for an event, saying paperwork can come later. The answer is no: later paperwork does not retroactively license a worked shift. The guard declines and offers to return once licensed.

Guard checklist:
- Identify the issuing authority for the city and role
- Verify current eligibility and application steps
- Complete required training; license before any post
- Armed posts: verify endorsement and qualification are current

COMMON MISTAKE: Treating a carry permit as work authorization. Personal carry and an armed endorsement are different authorizations. Only the licensing pathway authorizes armed guard work.

LESSON 2 — PI PATHWAY AND ASIS CERTIFICATIONS
A Missouri PI license is issued by the state board responsible for PI licensing, under the Missouri Division of Professional Registration. Pathway: verify eligibility with the board, apply, complete fingerprinting and a background check, complete any exam or qualification steps required, and hold the license before accepting client work.

Know the line: gathering information about people for a client and surveillance for hire are licensed activity. If a task may be PI work and you lack the license, route it to a licensed investigator.

ASIS certifications never replace licensing. APP (Associate Protection Professional) is entry-level, CPP (Certified Protection Professional) management-level, PCI (Professional Certified Investigator) investigation-focused. Each is earned through ASIS eligibility and examination. None authorizes licensed work.

CASE BREAKDOWN: A business asks a technician to follow an employee suspected of theft - "just observation." Surveillance for hire is PI work. The technician declines unlicensed and refers the client to the licensed pathway.

COMMON MISTAKE: Believing ASIS certification or experience makes the PI license unnecessary. Certification proves knowledge; the license is legal permission.

HANDS-ON EXERCISE
Sequence a plan for Maria, who wants armed guard work in Kansas City now, PI later.
Decision point 1: Maria owns a firearm, holds a carry permit, and a post is open tonight. Correct path: She does not work it armed yet. Complete the KCMO guard pathway first, verifying requirements with the authority.
Decision point 2: Maria's license is issued and she wants armed posts. Correct path: Complete the armed endorsement and firearms qualification through the approved process; verify both are current first.
Decision point 3: A client offers Maria side work following a subject. Correct path: Decline until she completes the Missouri PI pathway. Guard licensing does not authorize PI work.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
- Guard and PI work in Missouri are licensed - license first
- Verify current requirements with the issuing authority
- Armed posts need endorsement plus current firearms qualification
- De-escalation comes before any physical response
- ASIS certifications never substitute for a license

GLOSSARY
Guard license - City or state authorization to work as a security officer
Armed endorsement - Added authorization for armed posts, requiring firearms qualification
De-escalation - Voice, posture, distance, and time to calm a situation
PI license - State authorization for investigative work for hire
Issuing authority - State board or city body that grants a license
Certification - Credential proving knowledge, granting no legal permission

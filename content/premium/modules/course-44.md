The Sentinel Credential Standard
MODULE OVERVIEW
Level: Beginner | Time: 60 minutes | Prerequisites: None

LEARNING OBJECTIVES
After this module you can:
- State Sentinel's baseline credentials for every hire
- Describe the six ladders
- Explain approved degrees
- Apply the substitution rule and its exception
- Sequence a credential plan

LESSON 1 — THE BASELINE AND THE LADDERS
The baseline for every hire is CompTIA A+, Network+, and Security+, plus current First Aid/CPR/AED. Field roles also require a valid driver's license, and every hire must pass a clean background check.

Cyber ladder: CySA+, PenTest+, SecurityX, CEH, PNPT, eJPT, OSCP, GPEN, GWAPT, CISSP, CISM. IT/systems: Linux+/RHCSA, CCNA/CCNP, AZ-900/AZ-104, MD-102, AWS Cloud Practitioner/Solutions Architect Associate, AWS Security Specialty/AZ-500.

Installs: BICSI Installer 1, 2, Technician; ESA Certified Alarm Technician; Axis/Avigilon/Honeywell/Verkada training; OSHA 10; low-voltage licensing where required. Future guard: Missouri/KCMO guard licensing, armed endorsement with firearms qualification, ASIS APP/CPP. Future PI: Missouri PI licensing, ASIS PCI. Leadership: PMP, ITIL 4.

CASE BREAKDOWN: An installer asks to skip Network+ for a senior penetration testing credential. The plan is re-sequenced: without networking fundamentals, advanced material has nothing to attach to, and his current work needs that knowledge now. He completes Network+ and Security+ first, then starts the cyber ladder.

Ladder checklist:
- Confirm the baseline is complete or scheduled first
- Pick one ladder matching your role and next role
- Sequence fundamentals before specialty levels
- Pair each credential with field work; record renewal dates

COMMON MISTAKE: Collecting credentials in random order because a course is on sale. It costs more, retains less, and leaves the baseline unfinished.

LESSON 2 — DEGREES, THE SUBSTITUTION RULE, ITS EXCEPTION
Sentinel approves associate, bachelor, and master degrees in cybersecurity, IT, computer science, information systems, networking, and related fields, and an MBA for leadership paths. A degree is one way to prove capability, not the only way.

THE RULE: certifications plus proven field experience can substitute for a degree at Sentinel - except where Missouri licensing law requires otherwise (private security, private investigation). Where a license is required by law, no certification or experience substitutes for it.

Documented experience means work you can describe and show results for. Certification means the real credential, verifiable with its issuing body. In your plan, list what you hold, what the next role requires, and any license gate.

CASE BREAKDOWN: Two candidates apply for a future investigator role. One holds a bachelor's degree but no Missouri PI license. The other has no degree, but holds certifications, documented experience, and the required PI license. Internally, the second candidate's credentials substitute for the degree. At the legal gate, only the licensed candidate can do PI work - a degree never opened that gate.

COMMON MISTAKE: Reading the rule as "licenses are optional if I am experienced." It governs degrees in Sentinel hiring, never a legally required license.

HANDS-ON EXERCISE
Sequence a plan for Jordan, an installer with A+ complete, no other baseline items, two years of documented experience, aiming to move into cybersecurity.
Decision point 1: Jordan wants to start with OSCP. Correct path: No. Finish the baseline first - Network+, Security+, First Aid/CPR/AED - then enter the cyber ladder.
Decision point 2: Jordan has no degree and worries the plan is blocked. Correct path: Apply the rule - certifications plus proven experience substitute for a degree internally, so the plan proceeds.
Decision point 3: Jordan asks if substitution covers a Missouri PI role without the license. Correct path: No. PI work is the exception - the license pathway must be completed.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
- Baseline: A+, Network+, Security+, First Aid/CPR/AED for everyone
- Field roles add a driver's license; all hires pass a background check
- Climb one ladder in order, paired with field work
- Certifications plus proven experience can substitute for a degree
- Substitution never waives a license Missouri law requires

GLOSSARY
Baseline - Credentials every Sentinel hire must hold
Ladder - Ordered credential sequence for a specialty
Substitution rule - Certifications plus proven experience can replace a degree internally
License gate - Step where the law requires a license before work may be done
Proven field experience - Documented, verifiable work with results
Armed endorsement - Added guard authorization for armed posts, with firearms qualification

# 01 — Spot the Phish: The 60-Second Email Check

**Phishing = a fake email (or site) that harvests your login.** The "account locked" email is the most common lure on earth.

## The 60-second check
1. **Sender address.** Not the display name — the ADDRESS. `security@yourbank-secure.com` is not your bank.
2. **The greeting.** "Dear Customer" = mass blast. Your bank knows your name.
3. **Urgency.** "Locked in 24 hours!" Panic is the point — it stops you from checking #1.
4. **The link.** Hover (don't click). Does it go to the real domain? `yourbank.com.verify-login.ru` is not yourbank.com.
5. **The ask.** Banks never ask for passwords, PINs, or full SSNs by email. Ever.

## The one habit
**Never log in from an email link.** Open the app or type the address yourself. This single habit defeats nearly all phishing.

## If you clicked
- Change that password immediately (and anywhere you reused it).
- Turn on 2FA — app-based, not SMS, if you can.
- Watch the account for a week; report the phish to your bank and reportphishing@apwg.org.

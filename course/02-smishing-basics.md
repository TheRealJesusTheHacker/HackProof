# 02 — Smishing Basics: Don't Trust the Text

**Smishing = phishing by text message.** "Missed package," "bank alert," "toll due" — all designed to make you tap a link.

## The tells
- **Links from strangers.** Especially shortened ones (bit.ly, t.ly). Legit companies text from short codes, not random 10-digit numbers.
- **The fee trap.** "Pay $1.50 redelivery fee" — the fee isn't the scam, the card form is.
- **The reply hook.** "Reply YES to confirm" — confirms your number is live and owned by a human.
- **Codes you didn't ask for.** A text with a login code means someone is trying to log in AS YOU right now.

## The rules
- Don't tap links in unexpected texts. Open the **official app** instead.
- Don't reply to scam texts (not even STOP) — it confirms you're real. Block and report: forward to **7726** (SPAM).
- Your bank's real alerts never ask for passwords or full card numbers by text.
- Carriers, toll agencies, and the DMV don't collect debts via texted links.

## If you tapped
If you entered card details: call your bank now and get the card replaced. If you entered a password: change it everywhere you reused it.

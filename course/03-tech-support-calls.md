# 03 — Tech Support Calls: Hang Up

**The scam:** someone calls claiming to be Microsoft/Apple/your "security team." Your computer sent "error reports." They'll "fix" it — if you give them access and pay.

## The script
1. **The hook.** "Your PC is infected / your license expired / illegal activity on your IP."
2. **The proof.** They show you the Windows Event Viewer (always full of harmless warnings) and call them "viruses."
3. **The access.** "Install this remote tool so I can fix it." Now they're inside your computer.
4. **The payment.** Gift cards, wire, or "refund" scams where they "accidentally" send too much and ask for the difference back.

## The truth
- **Nobody monitors your home PC.** Microsoft and Apple do not call you. Ever.
- Real support never cold-calls, never asks for remote access out of nowhere, never takes gift cards.
- "Your IP was flagged" is meaningless — they don't know your IP, they're dialing thousands of numbers.

## The rule
**Hang up.** If you're worried, call the company's real support number yourself (from their official site, not the caller).

## If you gave access
Disconnect from the internet, have the machine professionally cleaned (assume passwords typed since are burned — change them from a clean device), and call your bank if you paid.
